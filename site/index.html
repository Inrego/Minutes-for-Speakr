Minutes for Speakr

A client for your self-hosted Speakr server

Minutes for Speakr is an unofficial Android, iOS and Windows app for Speakr, a self-hosted audio transcription and note-taking server. You point the app at your own server, and it records, uploads, and shows you the transcripts and summaries your server produces.

What it does

  • Record audio live, or pick up files from a watched folder and upload them.
  • Browse your recordings, transcripts and summaries.
  • Tag recordings, add notes, and set speaker counts and language before upload.

Where your data goes

Only to the Speakr server you configure. There is no backend operated by the app's publisher, no analytics, and no third-party tracking. The app's typefaces are bundled with it, so it contacts no font CDN either. The full details are in the privacy policy.
